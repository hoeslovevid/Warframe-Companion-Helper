How it works

Run beside Warframe in Borderless Windowed. Panels sit above the game and stay click-through until you unlock them.

  1. 01

    Install & launch

    Use the Setup installer, or run the portable build. Open the companion window.

  2. 02

    Enable what you need

    Toggle cycles, fissures, Baro, relics, and more. Arrange them on the Layout tab.

  3. 03

    Play with intel on-screen

    Hotkeys toggle the overlay and unlock drag. Relic scans fire from the reward screen.

Built for the session

One job per panel — live worldstate, reward picks, and local inventory.

Live worldstate

Cetus, Vallis, Cambion, fissures, Baro Ki’Teer, Nightwave, and arbitration timers with real countdowns.

Relic reward overlay

Scan the fissure reward screen (hotkey or EE.log). See set names, owned counts, and parts you still need.

Layout your way

Drag every panel on a mock monitor in the app — or unlock in-game with Ctrl+Tab and reposition on the fly.

Inventory stays local

Optional sync via warframe-api-helper or AlecaFrame exports. Data never leaves your PC for “needed for set” tags.
